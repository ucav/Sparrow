Aider is excellent and I used it for six months before writing Sparrow.
Three differences that mattered to me:

1. Aider is Python. The dep tree breaks on my machines about every quarter.
   Sparrow is one Rust binary that does not.

2. Aider's UI is the TUI. Sparrow has three peer surfaces — TUI, WebView
   cockpit on port 9339, and `--json` NDJSON — fed from one typed event
   bus. Crash one, the others keep working.

3. Aider has no `HardStop` primitive in its budget code. The cap is a
   warning. Sparrow's `--max-cost-usd` / `--max-wall-secs` / `--max-tokens`
   abort the run at the next safe boundary, no config trick can bypass.

What Aider does better than Sparrow: its diff-application strategy is
more robust than mine. I plan to copy it for v0.6.
